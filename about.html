About Martha Lamphere

A lifelong learner, mother, entrepreneur, health coach, and wisdom curator.

Exploring resilience, parenting, wellness, and human potential.through personal stories, research-driven reflections, and meaningful conversations.

The narrative

A life shaped by curiosity, conviction, and quiet resilience.

Martha Lamphere has always been drawn to the ideas, people, and conversations that make life more meaningful.

She is a builder by nature. As a young entrepreneur, she built a jewelry business that reached Bergdorf Goodman. As a mother, she has become deeply interested in what helps children develop confidence, self-trust, resilience, and emotional health. As a lifelong learner, she reads widely, studies deeply, and seeks out leading thinkers across parenting, wellness, longevity, meditation, education, and consciousness.

Her life has also taught her resilience. Through grief, challenge, IVF, motherhood, and the constant work of becoming, Martha has learned that life does not always give us a clear map. But it does offer us moments of choice.to give up, to harden, or to become wiser and more useful because of what we have lived.

Today, Martha is beginning to bring those threads together through reflections, conversations, and private gatherings. Her work is for thoughtful parents, women, and lifelong learners who want to live with more courage, consciousness, and wholeness.

Threads of a life

Six chapters, one story.

No single label captures Martha's work. Each thread informs the others, andtogether they form the lens through which she reads parenting, resilience, and human potential.

/ 01.Entrepreneur

A jewelry brand to Bergdorf Goodman.

Built a fine-jewelry business from scratch and saw it placed at one of the most discerning retailers in the country. The discipline and craft of building still informs how Martha approaches every undertaking.

/ 02.Mother

Parenting after nineteen rounds of IVF.

A long, deeply personal journey to motherhood, anda mother's view shaped by raising a gifted, sensitive, and self-driven daughter in an achievement-driven world.

/ 03.Health Coach

Certified, with a longevity and wellness lens.

Trained as a health coach, with sustained study across nutrition, longevity science, sleep, hormonal health, and the integrated practices that shape long-term wellbeing.

/ 04.Education Advocate

Board leadership at The Weiss School.

Active board service and advocacy for gifted, sensitive, and high-potential children.including supporting the vision for a high school pathway that meets these learners where they are.

/ 05.Lifelong Learner

Books, research, meditation, and meaningful conversation.

A serious reader and student of psychology, parenting, neuroscience, education, longevity, and consciousness.including a sustained Transcendental Meditation practice.

/ 06.Resilience

Grief, becoming, and the work of staying open.

The lived experience underneath the rest.what it has taken to keep building, parenting, learning, and contributing through the seasons that don't ask permission.
