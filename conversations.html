Part one.Conversations

Interviews worth keeping.

A small, curated series of conversations with authors, educators, doctors, researchers, and thought leaders whose work has shaped the way Martha thinks about parenting, resilience, and human potential.

/ Series in development · 2026

The first pilot conversations are being recorded.

Long-form interviews with the authors and thinkers behind the books on Martha's reading list.distilled into honest, substantive episodes worth your hour.

Each episode begins with a single question that has been turning in Martha's mind, then follows the conversation wherever it leads. Topics will move across conscious parenting, resilience, longevity, meditation, education, and the quiet work of becoming whole.

Pilot episodes are being recorded now. The first releases will be sent to the private list before they appear anywhere else.

"How do children really build self-trust?"
"What does parenting from regulation look like?"
"What does resilience look like when no one is watching?"
"What are the books we should be reading right now?"
Part two.Speaking

A speaker's voice, grounded in lived experience.

Martha speaks to schools, parent communities, salons, and small leadership gatherings.drawing on twenty years of building, parenting, learning, and resilience to leave audiences with something they can actually use.

Talk topics

/ 01.Conscious Parenting

Raising Whole Humans, Not High Performers.

A keynote for parents of gifted, sensitive, and high-potential children.

How parents can support self-driven, emotionally healthy children without confusing love for pressure. Built around the four core questions of the Raising Whole Humans series.

/ 02.Inner Work

The Inner Work of Conscious Parenting.

For parent communities and school audiences ready to look inward.

What we project, what we control, and what we are afraid of, andhow the parent's own self-reflection becomes the most important thing they bring into the home.

/ 03.Resilience

Becoming, Without a Map.

A personal talk for women, leaders, and lifelong learners.

On building, losing, parenting, becoming, and the seasons of life that ask more than we thought we had, andwhat the long road actually teaches us about hope and capacity.

/ 04.Wisdom

What the Books Are Saying.

A curator's talk for book circles, salons, and parent communities.

A distillation of the most important contemporary writing on parenting, attention, longevity, and the inner life, andwhat it tells us about how to live now.
