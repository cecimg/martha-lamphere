The Grade Was Not the Emergency.

A story about a missed assignment, a parenting choice, and the difference between controlling a child and trusting her to build her own self-worth.

My daughter forgot to turn in an assignment. The instinct most parents feel.the urgency to fix it, to email the teacher, to make sure the grade does not slip.rose up immediately. I noticed it. And then I sat with it.

The choice in that moment was not really about the assignment. It was about who was going to own her relationship with her own work. I could rescue her, and quietly teach her that I did not trust her to handle it. Or I could trust her, hold steady through the discomfort, and let her feel the small consequences of having missed it.

"I cannot buy you self-esteem. I cannot buy you self-worth."
